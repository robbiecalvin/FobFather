A few things to know

Before we meet.

Can you copy my building’s fob?

We copy compatible apartment RFID fobs. Tell us the type or brand when you request an appointment, and we’ll confirm whether we can help before you visit.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. Send an appointment request with your preferred date and time. We’ll reply to confirm availability and meeting details. Please wait for confirmation before travelling.

Is a digital copy included?

Yes. Every customer receives a free digital copy with their purchase. Ask us about delivery and use at your appointment.

Can you come to my building?

On-site visits in Downtown Vancouver may be arranged. Select the on-site option in your request and include your neighbourhood. We’ll confirm availability and any visit charge in advance.
